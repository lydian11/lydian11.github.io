# Solving Penchant Puzzle Hunt (2025)

This post contains unmarked spoilers for Penchant Puzzlehunt.

[Penchant Puzzlehunt](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/) was the first puzzle hunt I got do after having my puzzle interest rebirthed, and it *certainly* did not disappoint! It did come up right near the end of my semester, in a weekend I really could've really used to to actually study for the three upcoming exams in the following days, but at that point I knew my brain would be completely incapable of focusing on anything else because I *really* got hit by the puzzle autism high, so I just tried to get some of the puzzle obsession out of the way and leave some studying time for Sunday, can't say I recommend that strategy for my future self again!

I did make a bit of a silly decision going into this hunt, though. "10 feeders and a meta, surely I can solo this after a multi-year long hiatus of puzzling, what could possibly go wrong?" Turns out, more than I expected! I stared at the puzzles very intensely for about an hour, until i called it quits and joined my friends on the Blob Wizards. The only team that had vacancy at the time was the European squad, as the North America peeps had joined as a half-squad. But given I was having a pretty regular sleep schedule at the time (and I can't say the same for some of my Blob Wizard friends from the North America team) I think my timezone was probably effectively closest to the European group anyway! This gamble of trying to solo did end up costing me the initial ahas on some of the first few puzzles, but ultimately I didn't miss out on too much, and I'm glad I was quick enough to realize how unprepared I would've been to solo this hunt at that time!

Here are some puzzles I wanted to highlight from my experience:

### [Ladders](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/ladders)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">Thanks to my failed solo attempt I ended up missing this one as it was solved already when I'd joined, but looking at it from our Sheet I absolutely loved the idea, that's when it clicked for me like, okay, I think this hunt is gonna be *good*!</div>

### [Blank Matchmaker](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/blank-matchmaker)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">This was the puzzle I'd made the most progress while trying to solo (like 8% of it max), so I kept working on after joining fellow Blob Wizards. I thought I'd do a lot worse at this kind of puzzle due to not being one to keep up a lot with pop culture, but some blanks did jump out at me even without nutrimatic or OneLook's aid, especially Top Gun which I'd only thought of because there used to be roller coasters in the former Paramount amusement parks in the US with that name. Out of all puzzles I'd expect my roller coaster knowledge to come in handy on, this was not one of them! Eventually, I got stuck on the rest and my teammates took care of it the next day at early European hours while I was still asleep!</div>

### [Penchant Word Search Generator™](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/penchant-word-search)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">This was another one that had some good progress while I was away, and I ended up not contributing much to it as it was solved while I was staring at Blank Matchmaker, still, the idea here is really exciting and not something I would have expected to see at all from immediately glancing at the puzzle! I don't think it's fair to call a puzzle my favorite if I didn't really contribute to solving it, but this was definitely the most impressive on this hunt to me!</div>

### [Rereading](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/rereading)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">While this puzzle is relatively simple, I do like the construction flex here, a lot! This was pretty fun to solve along to, even if it a bit more on the trivial side compared to the other feeders on this hunt! It made me feel a bit vindicated having noticed how oddly the clues seemed on the first pass!</div>

### [Pen-and-Paper Logic Puzzles: An Introduction](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/pen-paper-logic)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">Our team looked at this one for a while, but unfortunately the errata made it a bit tricky to solve so it ended up being left behind and backsolved. I still want to shout it out though, because it's such a cool construction, and I really appreciate the idea behind it!</div>

### [Playing with Places](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/playing-with-places)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">Of course I completely forgot about the recommendation to look at the meta early! It's Saturday morning and my teammates had already realized that each answer could be paired with a Monopoly property (I helped narrow things down by noticing the curiously bolded first letters of the puzzle titles), but we were stuck for a good bit here after I try all different ways to place the answers on the board and a bunch of shenanigans. I go take a break to shower before going back to puzzling for the day, and then it hits me... "You count up all the colours and railroads." "Wait... There aren't 26 of these... Are there?" Spoiler alert: there are! I try to speedrun my shower as fast as I can to go back to my notebook and check if there were actually 26, but at that point I was kinda convinced on vibes already. Indeed there were and I get an answer, hooray, we're done with the hunt!</div><br>

Or were we? Turns out there are more metas! I go back to the info page and notice a very suspiciously worded "The hunt contains one round with ten feeder puzzles, and a meta accessible from the start." "Ah, they got me!" This was a pretty fun twist, and did explain the relative dryness of the first meta as the hunt finale. It's a great meta but it didn't feel like something that would motivate this whole hunt coming together, after unlocking Playing With Words and Playing With Cards, I now see what is!

After solving both we unlocked another two more metas, because of course we did! At that point it had dawned on me we were probably getting 5 metas to represent the circles on the logo, and I was secretly expecting a metameta to cap things out, but before that:

### [Playing with Cards](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/playing-with-cards)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">I think this was definitely my favorite meta from this hunt! There may be some bias involved here as this puzzle very fondly reminds me of <a href="http://2021.silphpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/card-sharks.html">Card Sharks</a>, a puzzle I've wrote! The idea of course, is very different, but I liked the idea of interpreting each letter as representing a different card and using the logic to work out who had what. It's a very creative idea and it works perfectly in the context of this hunt!</div>

### [Playing with Others](https://penchantpuzzlehunt.com/puzzle/playing-with-others)

<div style="margin-left: 30px;">I think we got the idea here very quickly, that each answer can be applied to the next sequentially, but even with the right concept we kinda stalled being unable to make some of them work. It got a bit late for my European friends and they went to bed, right before I was gonna do so myself as well I thought I'd might as well give the puzzle another look, right? It wouldn't hurt. Turns out while a teammate and I were solving Playing With Words earlier we kinda WOFed the answer and never finished the puzzle so we couldn't read off the diagonal. I don't know why it took us so long to go finish this, but I ended up fiddling with it alone and that was enough to crack the metameta. So I ended up getting the last solve while everyone else was asleep, oops!</div><br>

This hunt was a blast! It was just the level of difficulty I needed to get back into the swing of things but challenging and rewarding enough to remind me why I fell in love with this hobby in the first place, I managed to get some key breakthroughs on the first meta and metameta and that felt especially rewarding! All in all, Penchant is definitely a hunt I'll remember fondly, so many interesting decisions were taken here, all the puzzle-writing practices "from the future" like the separate leaderboards and saying in advance what were the puzzle genres used were very curious choices, but really the crowning jewel of this hunt is its structure. I've always been fascinated by overlapping meta structures, and this one was definitely one to be remembered! Making Playing With Places and Playing With Letters work together was really impressive, and the other metas are some very fine work of spaghetti! Ultimately the metas don't shine as much on their own by virtue of the structure, but I think it's very much worth the trade to have something like this, with the fakeout being especially cute! The feeders on the other hand, I thought were really interesting and provided some very cool innovations on the usual suspects of your average puzzle hunt. To be honest, I tend not be a huge fan of these kinds of "default puzzle genre but gimmicked"puzzles, but that's because they're usually played very by the book, and while that absolutely has a place for many audiences, it's just not something that wows me anymore. Penchant though, they did a complete 180 on many of these genres and made me really appreciate these kinds of puzzles in a new light with the creativity on display here!

Thank you for the Penchant team for sharing such an awesome and inspired hunt with us, eager to see what other puzzles y'all cook up in the future, hopefully we won't have to wait until 2054 for that!
