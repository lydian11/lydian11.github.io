# 2027 Latin America International Championships

## Introduction

This was my first International Championships and my second official event overall. While I was trying to fight the anxiety of wanting to score after returning empty-handed from Recife (bar a top cut on the Cup on Sunday, I suppose!) ultimately, the excitement of the coming tournament weekend was way higher. If Recife was the floor of my VGC experience and I already had such unthinkable fun, I could not imagine what LAIC had to offer to me, and even then, it exceeded my sky-high expectations.

The weekend and week prior was very uneventful though, I was way too nervous to get any significant practice on the free time I was lucky to find that weekend, and during the week I had a surprise assignment come up on Tuesday due Thursday, so I had zero time to practice on Showdown or even study my gameplans into the common teams. Thankfully though, I've been using the same team for pretty much the whole regulation, and after getting another top 64 in Latin America on the second Grand Challenge alongside my prior experience with the team, I was quite comfortable.

## Teambuilding Process

Speaking of which, my main rationale for keeping with this team was that of comfort; while in hindsight I would argue it was a good meta call into the tournament, with sun-rain and Volcarona Scarf Basculegion balance not seeing as frequent usage as earlier in the format; the fact was I did not have enough time to learn a new team, and no meta changes rocked the boat enough to make me feel like dropping Delphox Floette was worth the lack of familiarity I'd have with a different team!

### Mega Delphox
* **Ability**: Blaze
* **Held Item**: Delphoxite
* **Stat Alignment**: Timid
* **Moves**:
  * Protect
  * Heat Wave
  * Psychic
  * Nasty Plot
* **Stat Points**:
  * 10 HP
  * 12 Def
  * 12 SpA
  * 32 Spe

The EVs here guarantee OHKOing 2 HP Garchomp with +2 Psychic, surviving 0 SpA Modest Life Orb Gholdengo Shadow Ball in all but one roll, and surviving 28 Atk Adamant Life Orb Basculegion Aqua Jet in all but one roll. In hindsight I don't think this defensive roll is super needed because the only major team running Life Orb Basculegion is Michael Kelsch's Mega Charizard Mega Salamence team (as other Mega Charizard Whimsicott compositions like having the Life Orb on Garchomp instead) and that team usually runs Jolly Basculegion, but I decided the extra bulk was probably more helpful than extra Special Attack anyway, as that mostly helps getting better rolls to OHKO non-bulky Charizard at +2 with Psychic and Mega Raichu-Y with +2 Heat Wave.

### Mega Floette
* **Ability**: Flower Veil
* **Held Item**: Floettite
* **Stat Alignment**: Modest
* **Moves**:
  * Protect
  * Moonblast
  * Dazzling Gleam
  * Calm Mind
* **Stat Points**:
  * 10 HP
  * 25 Def
  * 16 SpA
  * 15 Spe
 
I was running a bit more bulk earlier to guarantee the survival of 32 Atk Adamant Kingambit and Excadrill Iron Head in all but one roll, but Chople Kingambit are tending to drop some Attack now to help tank Sneasler Close Combat better, and Excadrill's roll is a bit finnicky due to sand, so I decided to copy [Justin Tang](https://www.patreon.com/JustinTang/posts/delphox-floette-174406447)'s spread, now tanking 15 Atk Adamant Iron Head in all but one roll instead, and in return having more SpA to OHKO Rocky Helmet Indeedee with +1 Moonblast and get slightly better rolls into Archaludon. I was unsure if this was the right speed benchmark, outspeeding Glimmora didn't seem necessary given I should always be bringing Delphox into those matchups (unless someone invents Glimmora rain with Basculegion-F or something) and outpacing Adamant Excadrill also felt unecessary given how often it'd be under sand anyway and how useful the Trick Room mode can be against those kinds of teams. On the other hand, the benchmark of Modest Gholdengo also didn't feel very useful as the only set I could see running max Speed Modest would be like Focus Sash which I haven't seen since M-B. But I ended up staying on this speed benchmark as it also gets the jump on Jolly Mega Tyranitar and a general Speed creep against Charizard aiming to outspeed Venusaur in tailwind and Adamant Basculegion.

### Incineroar
* **Ability**: Intimidate
* **Held Item**: Sitrus Berry
* **Stat Alignment**: Careful
* **Moves**:
  * Parting Shot
  * Flare Blitz
  * Throat Chop
  * Fake Out
* **Stat Points**:
  * 30 HP
  * 2 Def
  * 8 SpD
  * 26 Spe
 
The Incineroar here is very fast to get the jump on Sash Kingambit, as Aerodactyl Charizard is a tough matchup, but that's usual on these kinds of teams, the only real thing I did here was make the HP divisible by 4 to maximize Sitrus recovery while keeping the same benchmarks of surviving Timid Mega Floette Light of Ruin 100% of the time and -1 Hisuian Arcanine Head Smash in all but one roll. I could've dropped speed to ensure that calc on Hisuian Arcanine guaranteed, but I thought getting the jump on this crowded speed range would be a good call. In terms of moves, Throat Chop always felt valuable to open up lines into Primarina, stop opposing Incineroar from clicking Parting Shot and having a backup Dark-type STAB which is crucial on the mirror, I never really missed any of the other options people were running here like Snarl or Helping Hand.

* **Moves**:

  * Kowtow Cleave

### Sneasler
* **Ability**: Poison Touch
* **Held Item**: Focus Sash
* **Stat Alignment**: Jolly
* **Moves**:
  * Dire Claw
  * Close Combat
  * Fake Out
  * Upper Hand
* **Stat Points**:
  * 32 Atk
  * 2 Def
  * 32 Spe
 
Sneasler here is also very self-explanatory. I've been running Dire Claw as I've been finding it so much more useful and able to bail you out in losing endgames compared to Poison Touch, sure, paralyzing a Milotic or a Whimsicott when you could've gotten a Poison is a bit unfortunate, but I think the situations where a crucial Paralysis or Sleep is what you need to win the game more than makes up for it. The 2 Def here helps with random Dual Wingbeat rolls, idk.

### Sinistcha
* **Ability**: Hospitality
* **Held Item**: Rocky Helmet
* **Stat Alignment**: Bold
* **Moves**:
  * Protect
  * Matcha Gotcha
  * Trick Room
  * Rage Powder
* **Stat Points**:
  * 31 HP
  * 14 Def
  * 21 SpD
 
Sinistcha is essential on this kind of team to support the Mega Delphox mode. The main change here compared to other teams like this is the Rocky Helmet, it's what I've been running since the start of M-B but after Recife with Mega Charizard Whimsicott teams and sand picking up, Colbur did seem quite appealing to help enable the Trick Room mode better against these teams, whereas the main impetus for this item before, breaking Hisuian Arcanine's Focus Sash was seeming less relevant. Still I felt like I didn't have enough time to test all the repercussions of dropping Rocky Helmet, and the few times I did I noticed it made some matchups like Golisopod rain significantly worse because it's a lot harder to get the Mega Golisopod in range of Mega Floette for the end-game, and I felt like I had great plans into Big 6-style teams and sand even without the Colbur on Sinistcha, alongside the fact that Rocky Helmet is generally more applicable for just chipping down things like Incineroar, Rillaboom if left in a 1v1 against Sinistcha, Basculegion, etc, it all just ended up making me feel like sticking with Rocky Helmet was the correct call, and I never missed Colbur Berry at LAIC. The only other deviations is running 31 HP, which lets me keep the jump point and the calc to survive Jolly Hisuian Arcanine Flare Blitz while getting some better rolls into special attacks, this is also why I'm running no speed, because I figured those rolls were more important than trying to win speed wars against opposing Sinistcha that literally never came up when playing this team. The main special roll to keep in mind is Life Orb Modest 0 SpA Gholdengo Shadow Ball, which is still more likely to KO Sinistcha than not, but just barely.

And with that, I had made day 2 in one of the hardest tournaments of the season! I think it still hasn't really sunk in, really. Right when I started playing VGC I did day 2 the Smogon VGC Major with this same team, in fact, but doing so in-person in this tournament that many of the best players fly all-over the world to attend, it did really feel special!

### Day 2

This was probably one of the hardest nights to sleep in my life, I find that I get quite anxious at these events, thankfully the anxiety doesn't get in the way too much of the actual games (or so I thought until now at least) but it was still quite difficult, I was also feeling very nauseated on the morning of day 2, but purely from anxiety of the coming day of competition. I hope with time I'll start to get used to the feeling of competition and things like this will pass, but until then I ate as much of my breakfast as I could and headed to the venue.

Pairings were first released around 8:30 local time, I saw I'd face the sole Mega Meganium in day 2, which was very funny after I called it a bad Pokémon despite this result on Discord the night before, so surely I couldn't risk losing to it! Turns out though that when Round 9 was supposed to start we had a to change play area and the pairings were changed too, this caught me by surprise but I saw I'd be facing Hippowdon Perish which I think is a good matchup for my team.

My first lead was Delphox + Sneasler to prevent Fake Out, obviously Kingambit is even stronger to threaten the Mega Gengar, but I am risking a Sneasler lead or a pivot into it turn 2 (especially with Rillaboom lead to activitate the Grassy Seed so it can move before Mega Delphox) which makes things dicey. That said, my opponent didn't go for either option and instead just lead with Hippowdon next to Gengar. The first turn Psychic was able to put Mega Gengar in sand chip range and its Shadow Ball in return did the same, I was able to safely reach an end-game where Sinistcha 1v1'd the Rillaboom. For game 2 my opponent kept the same lead and I decided to go with Mega Delphox and Kingambit, as only one could be Faked Out. I set up a Nasty Plot while threatening Kowtow Cleave and found myself in a very strong position against their Kingambit and Mega Gengar, but I made a series of misplays that opened the door to losing to a Special Defense drop on Kingambit. I protected Mega Delphox when forcing a Kingambit Sucker Punch to buy my own Kingambit a wake up turn was always correct as I had my own Sneasler in the back for their Kingambit and my own Kingambit could handle the Mega Gengar, the next turn I Kowtow Cleave'd instead of Shadow Ball trying to cover for a double Protect attempt to buy time (they couldn't Perish Song because I was up a Pokémon) but that was not necessary because my Mega Delphox was threatening a Heat Wave, so Heat Wave + Sucker Punch was always correct too barring a miss. Combining all those misplays with the Special Defense drop made my Kingambit drop. I think this was the only game that I really let the nervous get to me and I kind of stopped thinking, for all of these turns I immediately realized my mistake after locking in my moves, if I hadn't just panicked and locked in without thinking it would've been very doable to get the win on this favored matchup, but I didn't, so onto game 3 we go. We start with the same leads and I also do a bad play of doubling up the Gengar unecessarily (there's no point in doing that, if I am Kowtow Cleaving the Gengar Mega Delphox can always Nasty Plot). At another point I did find a winning line, assuming +3 Life Orb Kingambit Sucker Punch KOed their Sneasler (Rillaboom wasn't brought so no Grassy Seed), I was so sure of this headcalc that I didn't even bother to Swords Dance again when I had a free turn with Incineroar Fake Out and I was very sad to see their Sneasler survive the hit with 5% sealing the set. I don't know why I was so sure about this, I think I might've been basing my headcalcs off of Sash Sneasler which I've played a lot in practice leading up to LAIC. But yeah, a sequence of several bad plays where I threw a near-guaranteed win ended up making me lose this set. I was very devastated and frustrated with myself for letting my chance at Top Cut slip away like this, but the silver lining is after this I got my act together, the nervousness went away, and I started playing well like I'd been playing the day before. This set will stand as a very valuable lesson for me going forward, and I'm hopeful I can figure out plans not to get the nervousness get the best of me in situations like this!
